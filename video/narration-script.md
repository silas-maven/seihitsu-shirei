# Seihitsu Shirei narration

The guide uses one short narration clip per scene so timing can follow the voice naturally.
All clips use Verity X, a calm British narration voice, with Eleven v3.

## 1. Intro

This is Seihitsu Shirei—quiet AI help that stays beside your work, instead of pulling you into another app.

## 2. One-time setup

Start from the brain icon in your menu bar. Open Settings, add the key for your chosen AI provider, then allow only the features you want to use.

## 3. Ask anything

Press Option-Space to open the panel, type your question, and press Return. The answer appears right there in the floating window.

## 4. Highlight and answer

For a faster answer, highlight a question in any app and press Option-C. Seihitsu reads the selection and answers it automatically.

## 5. Read a screen area

If the text cannot be selected, choose Set screen region from the brain menu and draw around it. After that, Option-V reads the area and answers the question.

## 6. Ask by voice

You can also press Option-L and speak naturally. Your words appear in the prompt, and Seihitsu sends the question when you finish.

## 7. Choose answer speed

Choose Full for detail, Brief for one or two lines, or Blitz when time is short and you only need the answer.

## 8. Privacy

The floating H U D is left out of ordinary screenshots and screen shares wherever macOS supports it. Screen text is recognised on your Mac, and only that text goes to your chosen AI provider.

## 9. Recap

Remember four shortcuts: Option-Space, Option-C, Option-V, and Option-L. Everything else remains available from the brain icon.
